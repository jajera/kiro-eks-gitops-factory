## The map

<Still
  src="media/migrate-workload-map.svg"
  darkSrc="media/migrate-workload-map-dark.svg"
  alt="Eight-phase migrate-workload map with a gate on every phase: discover and inventory thirteen properties, containerise, publish the image to ECR, confirm cluster readiness, declare identity and secrets, generate GitOps manifests via a source-to-Kubernetes translation table, sync and verify on dev behind a human merge, promote and cut over at DNS, a named rollback path kept available until decommission, human-confirmed decommission, and the observability, alerting, and runbook work that follows cutover"
  caption="Eight phases, a gate on each, and a rollback path that stays open until Phase 8."

The gates are the design. Nothing starts until the previous gate passes, and a failed gate means
re-running that phase rather than working around it. Everything through Phase 6 is purely
additive, so the source workload keeps serving traffic and rollback stays a DNS change — right up
until decommission, which needs explicit human confirmation and is the one step that cannot be
undone with a flip.
