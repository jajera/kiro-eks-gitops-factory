<p>
  Three things carry most of the review weight. Only seven values may differ between overlays, so anything else appearing in one and not the other is drift. `app.kubernetes.io/name` is the selector key, so if it drifts between a Deployment and its <Tooltip term="networkpolicy" label="NetworkPolicy" /> the allow rules match nothing and traffic is silently denied. And `RollingUpdate` covers rollouts while a <Tooltip term="pdb" label="PDB" /> covers node drains — they are not substitutes, which is why the replicas matrix exists.
</p>
