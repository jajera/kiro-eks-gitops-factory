Two reviewer traps worth naming: a `sleep` loop shipped as a `worker` hides failures that a
`scheduled-job` would surface as failed Jobs; and queue-worker sync waves matter — a
`ScaledObject` that starts before its `TriggerAuthentication` comes up unauthenticated.
